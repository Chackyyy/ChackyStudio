Questions

Things people usually ask first.

What do I need to get started?

Your business name, a rough idea of the pages you want, and any logo, photos or text you already have. If you don't have them yet, I can help with that too.

Do I need a domain name already?

No. I can help you choose and register one, and it's registered in your name so it always belongs to you.

Can I update the site myself?

Yes. I can set it up so you can change text, prices and photos yourself, or I can make changes for you on a care plan.

How does payment work?

We agree on the price by email before any work begins, so there are no surprise bills. Usually you pay half to start and half when the site goes live.

Will it work on phones?

Yes. Every layout is designed for phones first and checked on phones, tablets and desktop browsers before launch.

Can you fix the website I already have?

Often, yes. Send me the link in your request and I'll tell you whether a few fixes will do or a rebuild makes more sense.

Ready to get your business online?

Tell me what you need. I'll come back with a plan and a timeline, and we'll agree on a price by email.
